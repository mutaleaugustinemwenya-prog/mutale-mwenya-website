The Author

About Mutale Mwenya

I am an author, writer and publisher interested in the fundamental questions of human existence: why we suffer, what gives life direction, how power shapes human behaviour, and what lies beneath the visible actions of individuals and societies.

I write to communicate life fundamentals such as the reality of pain and suffering, the necessity of purpose and vocation, and how the human psyche interacts with political power.

As a writer, I believe in writing about the truth that hides in the human heart and is acted out in the social arena. Truths such as malevolence, political violence, and the ego and its dark twin, the shadow.
